Privacy Policy

Rodriguez Case Manager — Last updated September 1, 2026

Rodriguez Case Manager ("RCM") is a case-management application used by authorized users of Rodriguez Law Firm LLC. This policy explains how RCM handles information when users connect services such as Google Drive.

Information RCM handles

RCM may process account information needed to authenticate authorized users and case-management information entered by those users. When a user chooses to connect Google Drive, RCM may receive authorization information and information about files the user selects or creates through the application.

Google Drive access

RCM requests Google Drive access so an authorized user can select files from Google Drive or upload files to Google Drive from RCM. RCM is designed to access only files used with the application under the permissions granted by the user. Files remain stored in Google Drive; RCM may store file references, names, identifiers, or links needed to associate those files with records in RCM.

Use and sharing of information

Information accessed through Google APIs is used to provide the requested RCM functionality. RCM does not sell Google user data. Information is not shared with third parties for advertising purposes. Information may be available to authorized users of the law firm as necessary for case-management operations and may be disclosed when required by law.

Security and access

Access to RCM is limited to authorized accounts. Google account permissions are controlled through Google's authorization system, and users may revoke RCM's Google access through their Google Account settings.

Data retention

Case-management records and Google Drive file references may be retained as needed for the firm's business and legal obligations. Revoking Google authorization prevents future access through that authorization but does not automatically delete records or file references already maintained by the firm.
